Discovery

Every engagement starts here. Before a single strategy deck or design file gets opened, we spend real time understanding the business, the market it's competing in, and the people it's trying to reach.

Start With Discovery

Overview

We start every project with structured discovery — stakeholder interviews, a competitive and category audit, and a review of whatever brand assets already exist. The goal isn't a checklist, it's a real understanding of where the business stands and where the actual opportunity is.

This phase sets the terms for everything that follows. Strategy, identity, and campaigns all get built on what we learn here, not on assumptions.

  • No guessing on positioning — We ground every recommendation in what we actually learned about the business and its market, not a template.

  • Faster alignment later — Founders and stakeholders who were interviewed up front rarely push back on strategy decisions later, because they already shaped them.

  • Real competitive context — We map where the category is crowded and where there's genuine room to be different, before any creative work starts.

  • Fewer expensive revisions — Catching misalignment in discovery is a conversation; catching it after a rebrand ships is a redo.

  • A record you can reuse — The research and interview notes stay useful long after this phase ends, for hiring, fundraising, or future campaigns.

Deliverables

01

Stakeholder Interviews — conversations with founders and leadership to surface what the brand needs to say and who it actually needs to say it to.

02

Competitive & Category Audit — a clear map of how competitors position themselves, and where the real gaps are.

03

Brand Asset Review — an honest audit of whatever identity, messaging, or campaigns already exist.

04

Audience Research — who the business is actually trying to reach, and what they currently believe about the category.

05

Discovery Findings Report — a working document that carries directly into the strategy phase, not a deck that gets filed away.
